CITY HALL
City Hall approves new patrol routes for SFPD
Y
Yavn· Today, 09:12
STAFF
Officers will cover the Marina and the Mission on rotating shifts from Monday, the mayor’s office confirmed this morning after a short vote at City Hall.
The change follows a month of complaints about slow responses to the Marina Lot car meets. Under the new routes, at least one patrol stays within two blocks of the lot from 8 PM.
Anyone clocked in with the City Government will see the routes on their duty map. Off-duty officers keep their old assignments until the next shift.
